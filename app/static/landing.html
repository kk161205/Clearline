Clearline

See where every payment stands, and answer disputes before they cost you.

Clearline is a console for small sellers who ship across borders on PayPal. Each order's money sits on one clear line, buyers get a page to ask before they dispute, and AI drafts dispute answers and invoices for the seller to review. Money only moves when the seller approves it, or under small refund rules the seller set in advance.
